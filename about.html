About Rihai Foundation

A Trust Built on Justice, Compassion and Freedom

Rihai Foundation is a charitable trust working for one simple but urgent cause - helping poor and undertrial prisoners secure bail. Across the country, thousands of people sit in jail for months or even years, not because a court has found them guilty, but because they cannot afford a lawyer, cannot arrange a bail bond, or simply have no one to speak up for them. We exist to close that gap. Our team works directly with prisoners, their families, lawyers, and courts to move cases forward, arrange bail bonds, and make sure legal support reaches people who would otherwise be left behind. We believe that justice delayed because of poverty is justice denied, and every case we take up is a step towards changing that.

Standing By the Undertrial Poor

  • We identify prisoners who need help, verify their case details with lawyers and jail authorities, and connect them to bail support, legal counsel, and a support network that stays with the family from the first hearing to the day their loved one walks free.

Our Approach

From Understanding a Case to Delivering Freedom

Every case begins with careful verification, moves through focused legal action, and ends with follow-up that lasts well beyond the day bail is granted. Through careful planning, honest communication with families, and transparent use of every donation, we turn compassion into practical, lasting freedom for those who need it most.

Our Mission

Our mission is to ensure that no undertrial prisoner remains behind bars simply because they cannot afford bail or legal representation, by providing bail assistance, legal aid, and support to their families.

  • To identify, verify, and support genuine cases of poor undertrial prisoners in need of bail.

Our Vision

A justice system where bail and legal representation are available to everyone, not just those who can afford them, and where poverty is never the reason someone stays in jail.

  • Our vision is to help 100+ undertrial prisoners this year secure bail and rebuild their lives with dignity.

Our Values

We work with honesty, transparency, and accountability, building trust with the prisoners and families we serve, and with the lawyers, courts, and partner organizations we work alongside.

  • Guiding every case we take up, so that trust is never misplaced and every donation reaches the people it is meant for.
