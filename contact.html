Our Location

Where We Make an Impact

We are based in Vrindavan, Mathura, and work with prisons, courts, and families in and around the region to secure bail and legal aid for those who need it most.

Rihai Foundation is a charitable trust that helps undertrial and wrongfully detained prisoners secure bail, access legal aid, and reunite with their families.
