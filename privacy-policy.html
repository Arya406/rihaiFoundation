Rihai Foundation ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using this website, you agree to the practices described here.

Information We Collect

  • Contact details you provide, such as your name, email address, and phone number, when you fill out our contact form or newsletter subscription.
  • Information related to a donation, such as the amount contributed and, where applicable, payment reference details shared with us for confirmation purposes.
  • Basic technical information, such as browser type and pages visited, collected automatically to help us understand how the website is used.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your queries, messages, or requests submitted through our contact form.
  • To acknowledge and keep records of donations made to Rihai Foundation.
  • To send updates about our work, if you have subscribed to our newsletter.
  • To improve our website and better understand the needs of the people who visit it.

Donation & Payment Information

When you donate via bank transfer or UPI using the details on our Donate page, the payment itself is processed directly by your bank or UPI app, not by this website. We do not store your card, bank login, or UPI PIN details. Any confirmation details you choose to share with us afterwards are used only to acknowledge and record your donation.

Cookies

This website may use basic cookies to help pages function correctly and to understand general visitor traffic. You can disable cookies through your browser settings, though some parts of the website may not work as intended if you do so.

Third-Party Services

Our website links to third-party services such as WhatsApp, Google Maps, and our social media pages (Facebook, Instagram, and X). These third parties have their own privacy policies, and we encourage you to review them. We are not responsible for how these third-party services handle your information.

Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell or rent your personal information to anyone. We may share information only where necessary to process a donation or query, to comply with a legal obligation, or with your explicit consent.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information you share with us. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security of information sent to us through this website.

Your Rights & Choices

You may ask us to update, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, or unsubscribe from our newsletter at any time, by contacting us using the details below.

Children's Privacy

This website is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. Any changes will be posted on this page, so please check back periodically.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please contact us at info@rihaifoundation.com or call +91-7906996070.
