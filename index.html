About Rihai Foundation

A Trust Built on Justice, Compassion and Freedom

Rihai Foundation is a charitable trust working for one simple but urgent cause - helping poor and undertrial prisoners secure bail. Across the country, thousands of people sit in jail for months or even years, not because a court has found them guilty, but because they cannot afford a lawyer, cannot arrange a bail bond, or simply have no one to speak up for them. We exist to close that gap.

Standing By the Undertrial Poor

  • We identify prisoners who need help, verify their case details with lawyers and jail authorities, and connect them to bail support, legal counsel, and family reunification.

Why Our Work Matters

Over 70% of Indian Prisoners Are Undertrials

Most undertrials stay incarcerated simply because they lack the financial means or sureties required to execute their bail orders. Rihai Foundation ensures that lack of funds does not keep someone in prison.

  • Direct verification of genuine cases through jail authorities and legal panels.
  • Financial assistance specifically covering bail bonds and court deposits.
  • Complete post-release transit and family reintegration guidance.

How We Work

From Identification to Freedom in Three Steps

01. Case Identification

We identify eligible undertrials through prison audits, family requests, and lawyer referrals.

02. Legal & Bail Processing

Our legal team arranges sureties, handles court documentation, and deposits bail bonds.

03. Release & Reunion

We receive the released prisoner at the jail gate, provide transit support, and reunite them with family.
