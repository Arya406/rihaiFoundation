Legal Aid

Family Support & Rehabilitation

Supporting families through a difficult time and helping released prisoners find work and rebuild their lives.

Prisoner Welfare

Making sure prisoners awaiting bail have access to basic necessities, medical care, and dignity while in custody.

Legal Rights Awareness

Helping prisoners and their families understand their legal rights and the bail process in simple language.

Prison Reform Advocacy

Working with lawmakers and courts towards fair bail practices and equal access to justice for all.

Our mission is simple - to make sure no poor or undertrial prisoner stays in jail only because they cannot afford bail or a lawyer. We help arrange bail bonds, provide legal aid, and support families through the entire process until the person is released and reunited with their loved ones.

You can get involved by donating towards bail bonds and legal fees, by volunteering your time or legal knowledge, or by helping us spread awareness about the cases we support. Reach out through our contact page and we'll guide you to the option that fits you best.

Yes, we welcome volunteers from all backgrounds, not just lawyers and paralegals. Training and guidance are provided so you can help with case documentation, family support, or awareness work, even with no prior legal experience.

Rihai Foundation is based in Vrindavan, Mathura, and our team works with prisons, courts, and families in and around this region. As our volunteer and lawyer network grows, we aim to extend bail assistance to more cases beyond our current reach.

We track every case from the day it is identified to the day bail is granted, and we stay in touch with families afterwards. Our impact is measured in bail cases resolved, families reunited, and people who go on to rebuild a stable life after release.

Cases usually reach us through jail visits, referrals from lawyers and courts, or direct requests from families who cannot afford legal help on their own. We prioritize cases where the person is poor, undertrial, and has no other means of arranging bail.

You can follow our blog and social media pages for real stories, case updates, and news about our work. You can also reach out to us directly by phone or email for updates on the causes and cases you support.
